**STEP ONE**

**WHAT TO BUILD**

---

* **PICK FROM THE 10 LISTED PLATFORMS**
Choose any of the 10 public-service platforms shown in the video.
* **OFF-LIST PICKS ARE ALLOWED**
Frustrated with a government site that isn't listed? Submit it anyway just know your odds dip if the judges haven't used that platform.
* **EXCEPTIONAL WORK GETS SEEN**
Build something truly outstanding and the team will notice on the list or off it.


**STEP TWO**

**A COMPLETE PROOF OF CONCEPT**

---

* **MOCK THE BACKEND**
Rebuild the platform end-to-end with mock data, a mock backend, and mock accounts. It only needs to work for the judges not at real-world scale.
* **MAKE IT WEB-ACCESSIBLE**
Host anywhere ChatGPT's new Sites, Vercel, your own stack. Judges won't download mobile apps: if it doesn't open in a browser, it won't be reviewed.
* **PROVIDE INSTANT LOGINS**
Include working test credentials so the team can log in and try the product exactly like an end user would.
* **PRIORITIZE THE USER EXPERIENCE**
Only the consumer side is evaluated the admin side is assumed. Real implementation comes later, if your project is selected.


**STEP THREE**

**IDEAS OVER CODE**

---

* **INTERFACES & INTERACTIONS**
Put your energy into the experience, not the plumbing. Implementation depends on each legacy stack that comes later, only if you are selected.
* **SHIP BOLD, USEFUL IDEAS**
A map, a tax calculator, a chatbot-driven flow the more unique but still useful to the end consumer, the better.
* **BUILD FOR BUSY CITIZENS**
They're frustrated, they don't have time, and they want a solution faster and simpler than the site they use today.
* **SKIP THE BELLS & WHISTLES**
Crazy 3D and Three.js showpieces look impressive but don't actually help the end user. Useful beats flashy.


**STEP FOUR**

**TEAM & EMAIL RULES**

---

* **TWO PEOPLE, MAX**
Go solo or bring exactly one partner that is the hard cap.
* **BOTH MUST REGISTER**
Your partner needs the official form too. Send them the link today.
* **ONE EMAIL, ALWAYS**
Your registered email is your identity for every form, result, and invite no exceptions.
* **SAME EMAIL IN BOTH ROUNDS**
Switch emails, lose access, or submit from an unregistered ID and the small team can't recover you at 5,000+ scale.



**STEP FIVE**

**FOUR THINGS TO SUBMIT**

---

* **LIVE PUBLIC LINK**
Host it anywhere - ChatGPT Sites, Vercel, your own domain, but it must open in a browser. No mobile-app downloads.
* **2-MINUTE DEMO VIDEO**
Minute 1: demo the product as a citizen. Minute 2: explain how you built it and why. Loom, OBS, or self-recorded teammates can co-present.
* **250-WORD SUMMARY**
Exactly 250 words on what it is and why it's better than the current solution.
* **PARTNER'S EMAIL ID**
You enter theirs; they enter yours the cross-match proves you're a real team. Going solo? Leave it blank.
